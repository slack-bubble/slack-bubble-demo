Add Slack Bubble to your website. Paste the snippet, reload your page, and say hello.

Your HTML embed

<script src="https://slack-bubble.com/bubble.js?id=T019JCM1D3R" async></script>

  1. Copy the script tag above.
  2. Paste it into your HTML file, just before the closing </body> tag.
  3. Save and reload your website. Your chat widget is ready to open.

This snippet uses the demo workspace ID. For your own website, replace T019JCM1D3R with your configured Slack Bubble workspace ID.
